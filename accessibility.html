Accessibility Statement

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026
Applies to Prime Traker (the "App", website: primetraker.com), offered by Money Maker Productions Ltd ("we", "us"). Please contact us by email at support@primetraker.com.

We want Prime Traker to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Conformance

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA . As of November 2, 2026, the App was tested with automated checks (axe-core 4.10, WCAG 2.0/2.1 A and AA rules) across its main screens, with no serious or critical issues found, plus manual keyboard checks.

Accessibility features

  • Text and button colour contrast meets AA ratios.
  • All form fields and icon buttons have accessible names.
  • Visible keyboard focus. Escape closes dialogs.
  • Touch targets of at least 44×44 px.
  • Animations, including the breathing exercise, respect the "reduce motion" system setting.
  • The page language is declared. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.

Known limitations

Charts are visual. Their key numbers are also shown as text in Insights and the Doctor summary.

Feedback

If you meet a barrier, email us at support@primetraker.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
