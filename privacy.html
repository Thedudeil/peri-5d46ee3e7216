Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026
Applies to Prime Traker (the "App", website: primetraker.com), offered by Maker Productions Ltd (company no. 516726080, Israel), Arye Brown 1, Petah Tikva, Israel ("we", "us"). Please contact us by email at support@primetraker.com.

1. Summary

Prime Traker is designed to keep your data on your device. Your symptom logs, treatments, notes and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not run accounts, and we do not receive, sell or share your health entries.

2. What the App stores, and where

  • On your device only: daily symptom ratings, sleep, movement, treatments (e.g. HRT name and dose), self-check scores, notes, reminders, language/settings.
  • Files you create: CSV exports, printable summaries and doctor letters are created on your device. You choose where to save or share them.
  • Purchases (if enabled): paid upgrades are processed by our payment provider. They receive your payment and contact details under their own privacy policy. We receive order details (e.g. email, plan, order ID) but not your health entries.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not load third-party fonts. If you agree, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, described below, to count visits to the website and the App.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.
  • We do not have access to your entries, so we cannot recover them if you clear your browser data. Use Export to keep a backup.

4. Website analytics (only if you agree)

We ask before loading any analytics. If you choose Allow, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views on primetraker.com, including the App. If you choose No thanks, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the analytics script is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the Visit counting setting in the App or the link at the bottom of each page.

We use these figures to understand how many people use the site. Cloudflare's product pages state that this service does not use cookies or localStorage, does not fingerprint people via their IP address, User Agent string or other data, and is not used to build personal profiles for advertising. We see only aggregated figures such as page views, visits, top pages and paths, countries, browsers, device types and referrers — not a profile of you as an individual.

When a page that includes the analytics script loads, your browser fetches the script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com) and sends a report to Cloudflare (cloudflareinsights.com). According to Cloudflare, that report includes the page URL without the query string, the referring page URL, and page-load performance timings. As with any request over the Internet, Cloudflare receives the request's IP address as part of ordinary request handling; Cloudflare states that it discards that IP address at the nearest data centre and does not store it in its core databases or logs. Your check-ins, symptom ratings, notes, treatments and other entries stay on your device and are not sent to Cloudflare or to us.

We see aggregated figures only, and they are available to us for about six months. Cloudflare acts as our service provider for this purpose and may process this technical data in the United States and other countries. Cloudflare participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Some browsers and ad blockers block this script, so the counts we see may be lower than the true number of visits.

Cloudflare's descriptions of this service are at cloudflare.com/web-analytics and developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics, and in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Hosting

The App is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation under its own policy. Cloudflare Web Analytics is described in the section above and is separate from hosting.

6. Affiliate links

Some "Comfort" product links are affiliate links (e.g. Amazon Associates). If you click one, the retailer may set cookies and process data under its own policy. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view, export, and delete all data in the App at any time (Settings → Export backup / Clear all data). Clearing removes everything the App stores except your Plus access code. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK, California, Washington, Israel) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact support@primetraker.com. The controller of any personal data we hold is Maker Productions Ltd.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Health disclaimer

The App is for educational and self-tracking purposes only and is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

10. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.
