Starter plan

Check with your doctor or a physiotherapist before starting. This is general, educational information, not a personal exercise prescription or medical advice. That matters most if you have osteoporosis, joint problems, a heart condition or recent surgery. Stop and seek medical care straight away if you get chest pain or pressure, dizziness or faintness, unusual breathlessness, or sudden sharp pain. If you have osteoporosis or low bone density, ask a physiotherapist to check your form on moves that bend or load the spine, like the bent-over row, before adding weight. Stop if anything hurts, and stay at a week that feels comfortable as long as you like.
