Simple starter moves

Check with your doctor first. This is general, educational information, not an exercise plan or medical advice. If you have osteoporosis, joint problems, a heart condition or recent surgery, ask your doctor or a physiotherapist what’s right for you. Stop if anything hurts.
  1. Sit-to-stand. From a sturdy chair, stand up and sit back down slowly, hands across your chest or on the armrests. Try 5–10.
  2. Wall push-up. Hands on a wall at shoulder height, bend your elbows to bring your chest toward the wall, then push back. Try 5–10.
  3. Heel raises. Holding a counter for balance, rise onto your toes and lower slowly. Try 10.
  4. Glute bridge. Lying on your back with knees bent, lift your hips, pause, and lower. Try 5–10.
  5. Standing side leg lift. Holding a chair, lift one leg out to the side and lower slowly. Try 5–10 each side.

Move slowly and keep breathing. Doing a little, regularly, is a good place to start.
