Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Prime Strong (the "App") is offered by Maker Productions Ltd (company no. 516726080, Israel), Arye Brown 1, Petah Tikva, Israel ("we", "us"). Contact: support@primetraker.com

1. Summary

Prime Strong keeps your data on your device. Your daily logs (strength sessions, protein and calcium-rich servings, vitamin D, energy, joint aches, weekly goal) are stored only in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, and we never receive, sell or share your entries.

2. What the App stores, and where

On your device only: your daily entries. Files you create, such as the CSV export, are made on your device, and you choose where to save or share them.

3. What we do not do

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not load third-party fonts. If you agree, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, described below, to count visits to the website and the App. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We have no access to your entries, so we cannot recover them if you clear your browser data or uninstall. Use Export to keep a backup.

4. Website analytics (only if you agree)

We ask before loading any analytics. If you choose Allow, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views on primetraker.com, including the App. If you choose No thanks, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the analytics script is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the Visit counting setting in the App or the link at the bottom of each page.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views on primetraker.com, including the App. We use these figures to understand how many people use the site. Cloudflare's product pages state that this service does not use cookies or localStorage, does not fingerprint people via their IP address, User Agent string or other data, and is not used to build personal profiles for advertising. We see only aggregated figures such as page views, visits, top pages and paths, countries, browsers, device types and referrers — not a profile of you as an individual.

When a page that includes the analytics script loads, your browser fetches the script from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com) and sends a report to Cloudflare (cloudflareinsights.com). According to Cloudflare, that report includes the page URL without the query string, the referring page URL, and page-load performance timings. As with any request over the Internet, Cloudflare receives the request's IP address as part of ordinary request handling; Cloudflare states that it discards that IP address at the nearest data centre and does not store it in its core databases or logs. Your check-ins, symptom ratings, notes, treatments and other entries stay on your device and are not sent to Cloudflare or to us.

We see aggregated figures only, and they are available to us for about six months. Cloudflare acts as our service provider for this purpose and may process this technical data in the United States and other countries. Cloudflare participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Some browsers and ad blockers block this script, so the counts we see may be lower than the true number of visits.

Cloudflare's descriptions of this service are at cloudflare.com/web-analytics and developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics, and in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Hosting

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) at primetraker.com/strong. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive your entries. Cloudflare Web Analytics is described in the section above and is separate from hosting.

6. Your choices and rights

You can view, export and delete all your data in the App at any time (Privacy → Export / Delete everything). Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK, California, Washington, Israel) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact support@primetraker.com. The controller of any personal data we hold is Maker Productions Ltd.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Health disclaimer

The App is for self-tracking and education only. The starter moves are general information, not an exercise prescription; check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.
