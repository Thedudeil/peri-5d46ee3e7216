Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. Prime Traker helps you track and organize what you notice, so you can discuss it with your doctor. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything, and it can’t tell you whether a treatment works.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. It runs in Safari and other modern browsers. On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen (Share › Add to Home Screen) so it works like an app and your entries are better protected.

What if I change phones?

Your entries live on your phone, so they don’t move by themselves. Export a backup file in Settings and import it on the new phone. The app reminds you to back up from time to time.

Can I use it if I’m on HRT, or not sure it’s menopause?

Yes. You can note treatments as you start them, and you don’t need a diagnosis to start tracking. Your doctor is the right person to talk to about what’s going on.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start your first check-in.
