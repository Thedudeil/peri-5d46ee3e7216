Perimenopause Tracker

A 30-second daily check-in that shows your patterns and builds a summary for your doctor.

How it works

1. Each day, tap 0–3 for how strong each symptom was (0 none, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 severe).
2. Watch your trends, top symptoms and patterns build up.
3. Tap Doctor summary before an appointment to print or save a one-page monthly report.

Your privacy

Everything stays on this phone, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. Use Export backup in Settings to keep a copy.

Important: not medical advice. This app is an organizing tool to help you record symptoms and prepare for conversations with your own doctor. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, or recommend any treatment, including HRT. Patterns it shows are simple averages, not proof of cause. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting, stopping, or changing any medication or supplement. If you have bleeding after menopause, chest pain, or thoughts of self-harm, contact a doctor or emergency services right away.
