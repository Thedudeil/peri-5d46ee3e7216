Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Prime Nights (the "App") is offered by Maker Productions Ltd (מייקר הפקות בע"מ, company no. 516726080, Israel), Arye Brown 1, Petah Tikva, Israel (בראון אריה 1, פתח תקווה) ("we", "us"). Contact: support@primetraker.com

1. Summary

Prime Nights keeps your data on your device. Your night logs (sleep quality, wakings, night sweats, evening tags) are stored only in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, and we never receive, sell or share your entries.

2. What the App stores, and where

On your device only: your nightly entries. Files you create, such as the CSV export, are made on your device, and you choose where to save or share them.

3. What we do not do

The App loads no analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts or fonts. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We have no access to your entries, so we cannot recover them if you clear your browser data or uninstall. Use Export to keep a backup.

4. Hosting

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) at primetraker.com/nights. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive your entries.

5. Your choices and rights

You can view, export and delete all your data in the App at any time (Privacy → Export / Delete everything). Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK, California, Washington, Israel) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact support@primetraker.com. The controller of any personal data we hold is Maker Productions Ltd.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Health disclaimer

The App is for self-tracking and education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

8. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.
