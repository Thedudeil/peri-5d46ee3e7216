Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026
Applies to Prime Traker (the "App", website: primetraker.com), offered by Maker Productions Ltd (מייקר הפקות בע"מ, company no. 516726080, Israel), Arye Brown 1, Petah Tikva, Israel (בראון אריה 1, פתח תקווה) ("we", "us"). Please contact us by email at support@primetraker.com.

This policy is provided for laws such as the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut's consumer health data provisions.

1. Consumer health data we collect

The App lets you record menopause/perimenopause symptoms, sleep, mood, movement, treatments (including hormone therapy) and self-check scores. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us. We do not receive anything you record in the App. If you buy Prime Traker Plus, our payment provider Whop sends us your email address, plan, order ID and licence status. Because Prime Traker is a menopause tracker, the fact that you bought it could suggest something about your health, so we treat this purchase data as consumer health data. We use it only to deliver and support your purchase, refunds and renewal reminders. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it except with Whop and our hosting providers as needed to provide the service.

2. Sources and purposes

Data you enter is used only on your device to show your charts, summaries, reminders and letters you choose to create.

3. Sharing

We do not share or sell consumer health data. If you export or print your data, you control who receives it. We do not place advertising pixels, retargeting tags or similar trackers on our website or App, and we never upload buyer lists to advertising platforms. If we ever want to, we will first ask for your separate, opt-in consent.

4. Third parties

If paid features are offered, our payment provider receives purchase data only, not health entries. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may receive technical request data.

5. Your rights

You may delete all health data in the App at any time (Settings → Clear all data). To delete purchase data we hold, email support@primetraker.com from your purchase email. To ask about any data we may hold, or to appeal a decision, contact support@primetraker.com. We will respond within 45 days. You may also contact your state Attorney General.

6. Geofencing

We do not use geofencing around health care facilities.
